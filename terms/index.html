Using Mipa

Mipa is a personal camera and photo journal. Import or capture images you have permission to use, choose a fixed flash look, add a note and create chronological photo page sets. You retain rights to your own photos and notes.

Page Passes

Each confirmed generation of a complete page set uses one Page Pass. Capturing, importing, editing and previewing do not use passes. A pass is deducted only after the output files and library record are saved. Cancelled or failed generation does not use a pass.

Purchases and storage

Page Pass packs are one-time consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Passes are stored only on the current installation. They cannot be restored after removal of Mipa or clearing its data. There is no subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple. Verified revocations remove only unused passes from the affected purchase.

Keeping your work

Save finished images to Photos if you want copies outside Mipa. Deleting an entry does not modify a completed page. Deleting a page does not return the pass already used. Device loss or removal of the app may remove its private files.

Availability

Camera and speech recognition depend on device capabilities and system permissions. Store availability and regional pricing are controlled by Apple. Mipa does not provide cloud backup or guarantee recovery of deleted local content.
