MIPA / THE FLASH JOURNAL

Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Your photos and journal

Mipa stores imported photo copies, capture dates, notes, drafts, finished page images and purchase activity in its private storage on your device. Mipa does not provide public profiles, a social feed or cloud synchronization.

Permissions

Camera access is used when you take a photo. Photos access lets you browse authorized photos and read capture dates; only the photo you select is copied into Mipa. Limited Photos access is supported. Add-only Photos access saves finished images. Microphone and speech permissions are requested when you open Speak Note. Audio capture and transcription start only when you choose Start Listening. Recognition uses on-device processing when available; otherwise Apple speech services may process audio. No voice recording is saved by Mipa.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. Mipa stores verified transaction identifiers and remaining Page Pass quantities to deliver purchases and prevent duplicate delivery. No payment card information is available to Mipa.

Optional permissions

Mipa requests notification and tracking permissions after you enter the main app. Their current status can also be reviewed through Settings. Mipa currently does not include advertising, analytics or remote push delivery integrations. Declining these permissions does not limit photo features.

Deletion

Clear All Data removes journal content, generated files, preferences, visible purchase history and remaining passes on this installation. A minimal list of previously delivered transaction identifiers remains to prevent the same purchase being delivered twice. Images already exported to Photos must be removed separately in Photos.

Contact

Contact address prepared for this product: mipa.app.contact.placeholder@gmail.com. This placeholder address must be replaced or activated before public distribution.
